## Physical pilot acceptance

Use the [pilot acceptance procedure](../../analysis/v1_1_release_20261012/pilot-acceptance.md) to record values, conditions and pass/fail evidence. None of these tests is completed by a CAD model or DRC result.

- [ ] **Inspect before power.** Check no-mask finish, thin-board handling, solder joints, fine-pitch shorts, correct orderables/orientation and unpowered connectivity. Hand-fit the flat capacitor after reflow and cleaning, with its outer polarity and center connection checked.
- [ ] **Establish safe solar and storage limits.** Measure actual cold panel Voc/I–V and BQ25570 input voltage/power; its recommended input limits remain unresolved. Measure charge plateau, both capacitor cells, midpoint stability, startup, leakage, cold ESR and radio-load transients. Review charging faults and large-load step behavior before accepting the balancing circuit.
- [ ] **Verify dark endurance and sunrise recovery.** Use debugger-free tests, actual temperature and full assembly. Measure residual GNSS current, reset/brownout behavior and stored-data recovery. Do not infer an all-night reserve from nominal capacitor energy.
- [ ] **Verify sensors and the matching firmware.** Demonstrate TMP117 ACK at 0x48, device ID and independent readings, then compare against a reference over cold starts and measurement temperatures. Confirm pressure, UV, IMU, GNSS and unavailable acoustic reporting. Pressure-sensor fallback is not evidence that TMP117 works.
- [ ] **Qualify the solar joints and bridle.** Record panel terminal thickness/wettable area, wire gauge, string material/diameter, bridle apex height and total mass. Compare flex, wear and joint security with v1 under measured loads and cold/handling cycles; use sacrificial samples for uneven or out-of-plane loading.
- [ ] **Compare installed RF and GNSS performance.** Record the exact regional wire cut, solder exit and payload geometry. Compare orientation-dependent links, GNSS C/N₀/fix recovery and local-transmit interference with v1 using the same settings. A VNA match alone is insufficient.
- [ ] **Accept the three pilots before the larger batch.** Record failures, rework and measured margins. Order approximately fifteen only after reviewing those results and a sustainable component supply plan.

## Deferred v1.1 experiments and supply work

- [ ] **Find a sustainable flat-capacitor replacement.** Preserve the current 30 × 14 × 3.7 mm rear envelope unless a measured improvement justifies change. Compare actual price/stock, terminal geometry, mass, capacitance tolerance, cold ESR/leakage and dark/recovery performance. Smaller 470 mF and wider HW203F candidates are tradeoffs, not equivalent replacements. Local working notes: `analysis/v1_1_layout_20261012/flat_storage/README.md`.
- [ ] **Resolve inherited IMU idle-current details.** Confirm LIS2DH12 SA0 pull-up handling (`CTRL_REG0.SDO_PU_DISC`) and the unused LTR390 interrupt configuration in the selected firmware. Hardware ERC exclusions document inherited connections; they do not prove the lowest sleep current.
- [ ] **Evaluate GNSS upgrades only against measured benefits.** Keep MAX-M10S as baseline. Recheck availability and exact firmware of MAX-M11N before a raw-carrier experiment; review pins 16/17 and actual power isolation. Compare MAX-M10N navigation/coexistence only under matched antennas and local LoRa activity. PPS access or reset alone does not provide supply isolation. Local working notes: `analysis/v1_1_layout_20261012/gnss_uv/README.md`.
- [ ] **Characterize the selected multiband UV experiment.** Measure channel dark offsets, saturation, temperature and angular response against calibrated spectra. A broad-band ratio alone is not an accepted ozone retrieval. The v1.1 part/population choice is an active release item above, not a decided deferral. [Current UV research](../../analysis/v1_1_release_20261012/uv_followup/README.md).
- [ ] **Qualify one-radio regional, wildlife and Meshtastic operation.** Measure the exact module front end, antenna and compatible channel/preset windows. Distinguish EU868/IN865 from 915/923 MHz choices. A wire change cannot bypass internal matching; one receiver cannot listen on all frequencies simultaneously. Local working notes: `analysis/redesign_20261010/radio_mechanics/README.md`.

## v2 investigations

- [ ] **Repack a shorter, potentially thicker board.** The same outline at 0.6 mm adds approximately 0.50 g in the existing model. Equal mass would require about 40.9 mm length at current width under proportional copper scaling, while retained component envelopes span over 52 mm. Produce an actual fit before claiming parity; compare upper panel mounts, center of mass and RF effects. [Materials model](../../analysis/v1_1_solar_attachments_20261012/materials/README.md).
- [ ] **Improve ambient-air temperature measurement.** Evaluate radiation shielding, thermal isolation and a remote lightweight probe against independent references. Separate chip accuracy from solar/conduction/low-pressure response errors.
- [ ] **Design an identifiable atmospheric GNSS experiment.** Select raw satellite observations or a separate coherent payload-to-ground instrument; specify antenna, logging, clocks and independent atmospheric references. Accurate position and LoRa packet timestamps alone do not resolve separate path temperature, pressure and humidity. Local working notes: `analysis/redesign_20261010/metrology/README.md`.
- [ ] **Develop ozone optics after reference measurements.** Compare broad bands with simultaneous narrower absorption/reference bands or a spectrometer, including out-of-band leakage and pointing/cloud/aerosol effects. Validate column above the balloon; do not relabel it local concentration.
- [ ] **Change RF and power architecture only from measured need.** A second receiver needs demonstrated wildlife/service benefit and accepted mass/power/cost. Integrate true GNSS isolation, logging and a qualified storage/solar solution when experiments establish their requirements.
