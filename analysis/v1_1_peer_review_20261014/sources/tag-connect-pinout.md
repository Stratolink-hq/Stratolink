# Tag-Connect cable pinout

Source: https://www.tag-connect.com/wp-content/uploads/bsk-pdf-manager/TC2030-CTX_1.pdf
Accessed: 2026-10-14. Official one-page PDF, Connections table. Browser PDF view was blank; PDF text reader fallback used.

The TC2030-CTX six-contact footprint assigns pin1 to VCC, pin2 to SWDIO, pin3 to reset, pin4 to SWCLK, pin5 to ground and pin6 to SWO. This applies to the CTX cable mapping; other Tag-Connect variants require their own cable/adapter check.
