# Primary source captures — 2026-10-12

The live JLC quote was compared through the public browser form, with no files uploaded and no order placed. [quotes.csv](quotes.csv) records the settled fabrication estimates; [quote-settings.json](quote-settings.json) records shared settings and capture hashes. The full text captures preserve each quoted fee. Browser button selection is not encoded in plain text, so use the explicit settings/filename together with the page text.

- [JLC quote](https://cart.jlcpcb.com/quote): two-layer FR4 TG135, 23.36 × 57.30 mm (or explicitly 40.90 mm compact case), green/white, 1 oz, ENIG 1 µin, single PCB, no assembly or stencil. Product category and deburring stayed at their UI defaults, Industrial/Consumer electronics and Yes. Fifteen boards at 0.4 mm cost $52.40; 0.6/0.8/1.6 mm each $23.40. The compact 0.6 mm comparison is $22.60. These prices exclude assembly/shipping/tax and are not a manufacturer-approved process quote for the new notch.
- [JLC capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities): see [captured specifications](jlc-capabilities.txt). Standard two-layer rigid FR4 starts at 0.4 mm; below 1 mm its thickness tolerance is ±0.1 mm. Routed NPTH slots are at least 1.0 mm wide with ±0.2 mm width tolerance; copper-to-routed-edge clearance is at least 0.2 mm. Plated edges and castellations need at least 0.6 mm material. This supports studying an unplated notch at 0.4 mm, not assuming the vendor has accepted the final artwork.
- [ZachTek SKU1044 panel](https://www.zachtek.com/product-page/copy-of-solar-cell-for-pico-balloons-polymer-4-8v-50ma): [captured text](zachtek-panel.txt) identifies nominal 4.8 V, 50 mA and 1.76 g. The page showed $14 each. No dimensional tolerance for the film or terminal strip was found.
- [ZachTek assembly guide](https://www.zachtek.com/_files/ugd/859ef0_bd82691b9a6e4c1fadce564cc29fc81d.pdf): the retained source is available as [PDF](zachtek-assembly.pdf) and [extracted text](zachtek-assembly.txt), with [provenance](zachtek-assembly-source.md). Its inner positive terminal slides into an open PCB cut; roughly 90 mm wires connect the outer negative ends to lower pads. Teddy's new inner-negative/GND arrangement deliberately reverses that orientation while keeping the panels in parallel.

The current TMP117 DSBGA lands already constrain the finish to a process suitable for 0.23 mm pads; increasing board thickness does not by itself make a cheaper HASL assembly equivalent.

## Product-category sensitivity

Selecting **Aerospace** in the same UI automatically adds a $16.81 four-wire Kelvin test for fifteen boards. With every other shared setting unchanged, the settled estimates are **$69.21 at 0.4 mm and $40.21 at 0.6 mm**. The thickness saving remains $29 per batch. These captures are `quote-aerospace-0p4mm-15pcs.txt` and `quote-aerospace-0p6mm-15pcs.txt`. Select the appropriate category and re-quote the final files when preparing an actual order; the default-category comparison is not a claim that the balloon qualifies for that category.
