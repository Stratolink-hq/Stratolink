**Status: accepted for the three-board pilot design review, 2026-10-13.** This signoff binds to the integrated green-mask/no-silkscreen PCB SHA256 `9d63500a087ef8d345c2337942106e6704d32ded953846883454bfad718adf6c` and schematic SHA256 `a06740b35d07d7b81999f7ace2a7a7ff02e185ca3e46a664630bd4bbd00c54b7`. The canonical `hardware/pcb` files were subsequently promoted to those exact hashes. There are no unresolved electrical/layout findings in this review. Manufacturing process and bench/optical acceptance limits remain below.

This review uses ams OSRAM primary datasheet DS001047 v4-00, design guideline PD001048 v2-00, Vishay primary Document28952 and the current JLCPCB capability table. Exact primary copies and URLs/hashes are in `source-ledger.json`. `requirements.md` records the manufacturer constraints, the independently read top/bottom pin orientation, selected precision reference, power calculations and optical limits. `mask-process-notes.md` records the user's superseding green-mask/no-silkscreen instruction and JLC's mask guidance.

The core electrical baseline is the pre-AS7331 canonical board SHA256 `8e2f83797dd68195aa71b73fc252fe57e53ccd3683ef5f74739dc4d25d6ce8d5`. `baseline-native.json` includes all 245 physical pads by UUID, not just distinct reference/pad names, so duplicated thermal and front/back solar contacts are not silently collapsed. ## Final review findings

- A repeated native CLI refill using the actual project and rules is byte-identical to the reviewed PCB and has zero DRC violations, zero opens and zero schematic-parity findings. `trials/refill-candidate/` retains the report and input/output hashes.
- All 239 retained physical pads and 227 retained schematic pins preserve their electrical connectivity; retained copper/paste pad geometry and RF feed geometry remain exact. The new 16-pin AS7331 symbol, board and project-local footprint agree with the independently read manufacturer package drawing. Only U6, C14 and C21 move; C23 and R27 are added. The exact placement and route changes are in `candidate-independent-review.json`.
- The 65 retained components preserve all 60 model entries, 236 body graphics and two custom-pad primitive groups. `check_retained_models.py` reads the frozen Git baseline, so promotion of the canonical board cannot silently turn that comparison into a self-comparison.
- U6 analog/digital supplies each have 100 nF local bypassing. The precision 3.3 MΩ R27 meets the primary reference-resistor requirements. The 5.502 mm reference trace remains on the component face; conservative projected I²C separation exceeds 2.21 mm and switch-node separation exceeds 4.66 mm. The closest opposite-face PB5 projection has a conservative bounding-box lower bound of 0.146 mm; PB5 is a status signal, not a continuously switching clock. This is acceptable for the pilot layout, with leakage, supply-noise and repeatability checks still required on the populated sensor.
- Ground return continuity was checked as physical copper islands, not inferred from shared net labels. Two local 0.125 mm ground necks clear adjacent signals under the existing fabrication constraints without a new width waiver. Every added via is 0.5 mm copper/0.3 mm drill. Right-bank UV ground is explicitly strapped to H2; the rejected rear-via shortcut intersected the rear solar supply and is not included.
- Maximum package outlines leave 0.315 mm between U6 and R27, 1.13 mm between U6 and TMP117, and 1.153 mm between R27 and the H2 hole. These exclude placement tolerance, solder fillets and the unknown string shape. All three via annuli intersecting the maximum U6 body envelope are tented on both faces; the separate actual-Gerber proof checks the entire annulus, including the corner GND via. Minimum front-mask overlap beyond those annuli is 0.141 mm.
- Final SDA routed length decreases by 3.685 mm and SCL increases by 1.496 mm. At nominal 4.7 kΩ pull-ups, the AS7331 300 ns rise-time limit permits about 75.3 pF under a lumped RC approximation. Actual assembled rise time must be measured; trace length alone does not qualify the bus.
- The sampled GNSS rear-ground comparison has zero lost and eight gained samples. Exact polygon subtraction finds 0.0000623 mm² of lost boundary slivers, all within 2 µm of the new boundary, and 0.0180 mm² gained. The raw detector intentionally reports that change for review; the reviewed disposition is insignificant refill-boundary variation, with no new return-path cut and no changed local RF copper primitives. The green-mask dielectric still warrants the normal RF/GNSS bench check.
- Existing modeled solar panels and their lead wires clear the ±10° normal optical cone, with a conservative nearest obstruction angle of 24.38°. This is a model clearance screen, not an implemented angle-limiting optical system or measured flight assembly.

`final-review.json` records the dispositions and evidence hashes. `static-geometry-scope.json` binds to the preceding electrical-only freeze; the integrated finish is separately checked in `../finish/` and `../manufacturing/`. ## Reproduce the frozen review

Use `/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.9/bin/python3.9` for scripts importing native `pcbnew`. The other scripts use ordinary Python3. Commands below are relative to this directory; supply the actual immutable candidate path.

1. `python3 prepare_refill.py --project CANDIDATE --name candidate` makes a scratch copy and fills it with the native CLI using the actual project/rules. Copies live under `~/Repositories/ai-working/scratch/stratolink-release-20261012/as7331/review/`. Then run `native_snapshot.py --project CANDIDATE --refilled-pcb SCRATCH/refill-candidate/stratolink.kicad_pcb --output candidate-native.json` with KiCad Python. This checks every explicit source track/via net name against the refilled native result; a stale-fill net reassignment fails.
2. Export a fresh `candidate-netlist.xml` with `kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml --output candidate-netlist.xml CANDIDATE/stratolink.kicad_sch`.
3. `python3 compare_candidate.py` for UV-only scope or `python3 compare_candidate.py --allow-green-finish` for the expressly authorized integrated finish. The latter allows F.Mask/B.Mask pad-layer changes only; copper/paste layers remain exact.
4. `check_uv_library.py --candidate CANDIDATE` with KiCad Python.
5. `check_rule_scope.py --candidate CANDIDATE --allow-green-finish` checks project/rules scope, including only the two expressly selected mask-related project changes. Omit the flag for the earlier UV-only candidate.
6. `gnss_return_sample.py --pcb CANDIDATE/stratolink.kicad_pcb --refilled-pcb SCRATCH/refill-candidate/stratolink.kicad_pcb --output candidate-gnss-return.json`, `analog_screen.py --pcb ...`, and `mechanical_screen.py --pcb ...` with KiCad Python. Then run `python3 compare_gnss_return.py`.
7. Run `gnss_ground_boolean.py` with KiCad Python after the saved-fill inputs have been created. Its raw status flags any exact polygon loss; assess the recorded areas and boundary-distance screen rather than ignoring the flag. Run `check_retained_models.py --candidate CANDIDATE` with KiCad Python.
8. `python3 optical_envelope.py` after the native snapshot. This checks the existing solar/lead model against the sensor normal cone; it does not provide angle-rejection optics.
9. Re-export and visually inspect the schematic PDF and the final copper/mask/paste plots; run DRC/ERC and package population/stencil checks on the exact final hashes.

The optical envelope uses existing photo-scaled solar models; real panel bends and lashing geometry are not measured. The known suspension line is a thin stretchy triangle through both corner holes, with unknown diameter and actual trajectory.

Full trial projects and saved refill boards remain in the scratch location above. Their reports and hashes are retained in `trials/`; `scratch-relocations.json` records the move. The failed short right-bank via experiment is explicitly rejected because it intersects the rear solar supply; it is not a release candidate.

## Explicit limits

AS7331 supplies separately filtered UVA/UVB/UVC channels. The board integration does not establish an ozone accuracy claim. Manufacturer guidance limits filter incidence to ±10 degrees and warns that VIS/NIR solar leakage can dominate weak UV channels. Useful ozone-column retrieval requires a calibrated optical path, angular response, stray-light rejection and flight validation. The assembled sensor must remain within its −40…85°C operating range; altitude alone does not qualify cold die operation. Raw bands/settings should be retained.

Native connectivity and DRC establish modeled geometry, not solder quality, exact I²C rise time, antenna match, cold operation, actual string/panel fit or calibration. Future U6 population also requires stencil and MSL handling, a clean optical aperture, and inspection of tent integrity beneath and at the edge of the package. The 0.35 mm lands and 0.30 mm paste apertures are derived from the manufacturer's terminal dimensions; they are not a manufacturer-qualified land pattern. Factory CAM must retain the reviewed fine-pitch mask geometry and tenting. Pilot population omits only U6 and its paste apertures; C14, C23 and R27 remain installed. The full canonical footprint retains all paste apertures for future sensor population.
