# Scoped courtyard proposal

This is an intermediate geometry proposal, not a routing signoff. `propose_courtyards.py` modifies only U5/R27 courtyard graphics and, when selected, the explicit R27 placement. It checks that all pad, model, body graphics and other footprint fields remain unchanged. It was applied only to the separate `courtyard-trial/` copy; source CAD was not changed by the reviewer.

U5's inherited courtyard extends about 1 mm beyond the body on each side. The replacement rectangle uses the TI **maximum** YBG outline, 0.98 × 1.518 mm, plus 0.25 mm per side: local coordinates ±0.740 × ±1.009 mm. This retains more allowance than using the nominal F.Fab drawing. The existing balls, copper, mask, model and corrected TMP117 pin mapping remain intact.

R27's standard 0805 courtyard was a rectangular envelope with larger unused corners. The proposed octagon contains the union of the actual two rounded PCB lands and the Vishay maximum package outline, offset outward by at least 0.125 mm. Its extrema are ±1.550 × ±0.825 mm with 0.210 mm diagonal corner cuts. Lands remain 1.025 × 1.400 mm, centered at ±0.9125 mm with 0.250 mm corner radius. The Vishay maximum body is 2.1 × 1.4 mm. The octagon gives 0.125 mm minimum side allowance and 0.131838 mm minimum diagonal allowance relative to a rounded land. A 0.220 mm chamfer would slightly cut inside the requested offset envelope, so it is not used.

The trial places R27 at (71.9, 34.08) mm, 90 degrees, 0.22 mm north of the prior candidate. Project-aware native DRC reports **no courtyard overlaps** with this proposal, including H2, C19, U6 and U5/C23. Maximum-outline screens give U6/R27 separation 0.31468 mm, R27-to-H2 copper annulus 0.55262 mm and R27-to-H2 drilled hole 1.15262 mm. The raw DRC and source/output hashes are retained in `trials/courtyard-trial/`; the full project is under the scratch path recorded in `scratch-relocations.json`. Other routing faults were present in that deliberately intermediate copy, including the R27 route becoming stale when the part moved; they must be resolved in the final candidate.

These are explicit project-specific assembly envelopes, not a claim of a particular IPC density level or manufacturer-recommended footprint. They do not include an unknown suspension string, solder fillet growth, placement error or assembly equipment access. The string's actual diameter and path remain unmeasured. Final order artwork and real assembly must be reviewed with the final routed placement.
