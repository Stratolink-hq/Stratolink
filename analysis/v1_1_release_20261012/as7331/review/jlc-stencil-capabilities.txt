How do I choose the right stencil thickness for my PCB?
You have two options. Option 1: Select the thickness yourself based on your component pitch and standoff height (commonly 0.10–0.12 mm for fine-pitch BGA/QFN; 0.15–0.18 mm for general SMT; 0.20 mm+ for boards with large connectors or power components). Option 2: Let JLCPCB engineers select the appropriate thickness for you based on your Gerber file features. Either option appears as a configuration choice on the ordering page.
