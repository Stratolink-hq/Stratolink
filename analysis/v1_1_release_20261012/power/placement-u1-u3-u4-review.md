# U1 / U3 / U4 placement review

Read-only review of canonical PCB SHA-256 `490af47db22ef076938c17319782f194623d8f8b6a3f864a2e0cc23b3d025b15`. Numbered pads and placement centers were extracted through native KiCad, independently of the historical CPL. The source and output CSV hashes, every relevant pad, and center assertions are in `placement-u1-u3-u4-native.json`; reproduce with KiCad's bundled Python and `review_placement_u1_u3_u4.py`.

All three original CPL centers equal the native SMD copper bounding centers exactly at the six-decimal precision exported. Package land geometry is symmetric around these centers. Bottom-view visualization mirrors board X, whereas the CSV keeps global board X and negates board Y. There is no evidence supporting a CAD or CPL XY offset. Supplier rotation corrections belong to its part-model convention; none requires rotating the actual footprint or changing routing.

U1's native pad 1 is `(65.540999, 38.814998)`, southwest of its center in the top-through-board coordinate system. Viewed from the bottom with north up, it is lower-right. [TI Rev G, p4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25570.pdf) explicitly shows the dot next to terminal 1, with 1–5 on one side and terminal 20 adjacent across the corner. The native pad ordering agrees after this bottom-side transform. `jlc/probe-u1-90-crop.png` now has both the package's black dot and the supplier's external purple indicator at lower-right, correctly aligned. The larger selected rectangle extends beyond the rendered body and is not a placement-center datum. In the earlier close-up, nearby C1/C2 spacing gives approximately 33 pixels/mm; their centers predict U1's body center within a few pixels, not the roughly 1 mm shift suggested by the selected rectangle.

U3's pad 1 is `(70.286999, 52.813999)`, lower-right on the front. [u-blox UBX-20035208 R08, Figure 2 on p9](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/MAX-M10S_DataSheet_UBX-20035208.pdf) is explicitly a top view with terminal 1 at lower-right, terminal 9 upper-right, terminal 10 upper-left and terminal 18 lower-left. All four corners agree with the canonical footprint and the current supplier indicator. Generic red reference text orientation is not a pin-numbering datum.

U4's pad 1/VDD is `(69.823, 56.933)`, upper-left; pad 8/SCLK is upper-right, pad 4/CSB lower-left and pad 5/CSB lower-right. The manufacturer's graphical top view explicitly has this numbering and a 3×5 mm body with 1.25 mm vertical pad pitch: [Measurement Specialties MS5611-01BA03, Oct 26 2012, p14, manufacturer PDF hosted by a distributor](https://robu-prod-media.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2019/07/GY-63-MS5611-01BA03-High-Precision-Pressure-Sensor.pdf). The current [TE June 2017 datasheet, p15–16](https://www.te.com/commerce/DocumentDelivery/DDEController?Action=srchrtrv&DocFormat=pdf&DocLang=English&DocNm=MS5611-01BA03&DocType=Data+Sheet&PartCntxt=MS561101BA03-50) has the same electrical pin table and package dimensions. The older PDF was used to visually check the numbered package drawing because direct downloads of the current TE PDF returned HTTP403 and the web screenshot wrapper did not deliver image content. Its cached SHA-256 is `908f9766e99cf0b1719815d9a4a52e588e8658c48e6d3110ac50417fddc5b53a`.

**The black circle on the supplier's U4 body is a pressure-port depiction, not its pin-1 mark.** In `jlc/probe-u4-90-crop.png` the long axis is vertical but the external purple pin-1 indicator is lower-right: that 90° setting is wrong. In the original CPL0 preview its purple indicator is lower-left. Parent's controlled supplier reupload demonstrated that increasing CPL rotation by90° rotates the supplier depiction counterclockwise on both board sides. Thus CPL270 rotates the original lower-left indicator clockwise into upper-left, while making the body vertical. The controlled CPL270 reimport in `jlc/probe-u4-270-crop.png` now shows exactly that: the external purple pin-1 indicator is upper-left, the 5 mm body axis is vertical, and the pressure port is below center. This probe passes the intended orientation check. Do not use its black pressure port or generic reference text to infer pin1.

No canonical CAD or placement CSV was edited by this review. Historical CPL agreement was not treated as proof of supplier model orientation.

Final controlled-probe evidence: `placement-u1-u3-u4-probe-attestation.json` records the exact source CSV `/Users/twarn/Repositories/ai-working/scratch/stratolink-release-20261012/supplier-rotation-review/CPL-pin1-reviewed-probe.csv` (SHA-256 `eb8ef43bb3b1a6ee0b1efc3f3c1280f519686b6ba1fda44e61ace8cc6c4d1069`) and screenshot hashes. U1=90°, U3=180°, U4=270° are accepted for this supplier probe with the original XY centers retained. The later generated manufacturing package must preserve those reviewed rows.
