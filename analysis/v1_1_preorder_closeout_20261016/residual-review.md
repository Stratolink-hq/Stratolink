## Bounds that do and do not settle the review

The old 8.25 MΩ charge divider had a numerical rating violation in a valid room-temperature screen. That was a real design defect and was corrected to 7.50 MΩ. The retained source does not expose a comparable decisive new violation that mandates a CAD change. The current 52% initial split follows CAP-XX's dual-cell matching discussion, rather than assuming arbitrary independent ±20% cell capacitances. It remains a screening model, not an all-temperature guarantee for the actual capacitor.

The modeled 36.8 Ω uniform RF section does not by itself demonstrate unacceptable GNSS loss. For an ideal lossless uniform section between equal 50 Ω ports, transmission is `1 / [1 + ((Z/50 - 50/Z)^2 / 4) sin²(theta)]`. At 36.8 Ω its mismatch insertion loss is at most about **0.40 dB** for any electrical length. This bounded calculation excludes real dielectric/conductor losses, diode branch, pad transitions, finite grounds and the antenna, so it neither certifies the assembled link nor establishes a reason to disrupt the engineer's reroute before prototype measurement.

Solar input has no equivalent closed bound in the existing panel documentation. The historical 5.538 V ADC report is uncalibrated and is not proof that the BQ input reached that voltage; it is also not evidence that the input is safe. No desktop simulation can supply missing panel cold-I–V data. The appropriate remaining test is explicit in the pilot acceptance procedure: first power from a current-limited, rated source, then measure the real panels and VIN before unrestricted outdoor qualification.
